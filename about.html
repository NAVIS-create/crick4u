Our Story

Built by Cricketers,
Engineered for Precision

Historically, broadcast-level ball tracking, wagon wheels, pitch heatmaps, and automated video tagging were a luxury reserved strictly for elite national teams with multi-million dollar budgets.

Local clubs, school cricket academies, independent coaches, and emerging tournament organizers were left with manual scorebooks, cumbersome mobile video recordings, and hours of tedious manual video editing after every match.

Crick4U was founded to break that barrier. We set out to engineer an AI-powered cricket analysis software and affordable camera setup that could turn raw video into frame-by-frame match insights within seconds — directly from the ground.

🎯

Our Core Mission

To democratize professional cricket technology so that every talent, coach, and tournament — regardless of budget or location — has access to broadcast-grade analytics to unlock their true potential.

What We Do

The Complete Cricket Analysis Toolkit

Everything you need to capture, tag, analyze, and review cricket matches in real time.

Computer Vision Ball Tracking

Our algorithms track ball trajectory, speed, pitch line, bounce point, and deviation angles with 100% accuracy.

Instant Video Clipping & Replay

Every delivery is automatically cut and categorized by batter, bowler, shot type, and dismissal for instant playback during breaks.

Interactive Wagon Wheels & Heatmaps

Visualize run scoring zones, dot-ball clusters, boundary patterns, and bowler release release angles on high-resolution field graphs.

Automated PDF Match Dossiers

Export executive match reports, player scorecards, and coaching summaries with 1 click to share via WhatsApp or Email.

Ground Camera Setup & Rigs

We provide full hardware setups ($75/day) or cost-effective 1-side camera setups with certified operators ready on match day.

Proprietary Strategy Security

Your team's recordings, tactical weakness notes, and opponent scouting logs are 100% private and protected by strict encryption.
