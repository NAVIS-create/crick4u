Features

Everything You Need to Analyze Cricket

From live match scoring to cloud video analytics, Impact Play gives coaches, analysts, and broadcasters the complete toolkit.

PostGIS Spatial Analytics

Visualize pitch maps, wagon wheels, beehive plots, and ball-by-ball stats powered by PostGIS spatial engine.

Video Capture & Tagging

Integrated video capture widget with automated delivery clipping. Tag, review, and replay every ball — locally or in the cloud.

Collaborative Scoring

Multiple scorers can work simultaneously on a live match. Real-time sync ensures every ball is captured accurately.

Team Management & Viewer Portals

Manage teams, players, and fixtures. Dedicated player viewer portals with video search available on Analyzer Pro+ tier.

KPI & Match Reports

Generate ball-by-ball reports, batting & bowling KPIs, fielding stats, and post-match summaries ready for coaches and broadcasters.

Automated Live Scoring Overlay

Broadcast-ready live scoring overlays that update in real-time. Dedicated server scaling handles massive concurrent fan viewers.
